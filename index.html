About

Cursor Time is a library of cursors in an After Effects panel. Pick one, press Create Cursor, and it lands in your comp as a single shape layer with its point exactly on the layer's anchor.

Animate Position to move it, and keyframe its State to hover, click, grab or wait. The click ripple and the press squash fire on their own whenever it clicks. Colors, size and shadow are all controls in Effect Controls, so you can change them at any time.

Setup

  1. Download Cursor Time. It's one CursorTime_v0.1.0.zxp file for both Mac and Windows.
  2. Install it. Open the .zxp with a ZXP installer (aescripts ZXP Installer is free, for Mac and Windows).
  3. Open the panel. Restart After Effects and choose Window → Extensions → Cursor Time.
  4. Create a cursor. Open a comp, pick a cursor and press Create Cursor (or double-click it). It lands in the middle of the comp, ready to animate.
  5. Animate it. Keyframe Position to move it and State (in Effect Controls → Cursor Time) to hover and click.

If the installer warns you

Cursor Time is signed with a self-signed certificate, so your ZXP installer may show an "unverified publisher" notice. That's expected; go ahead and install.

Advanced and uninstalling

Where things go

Each new cursor gets its Cursor Time controls from a small preset that the panel writes here:

~/Library/Application Support/CursorTime/presets
%APPDATA%\CursorTime\presets

Cursors you've already created don't need it: the controls are saved in your project.

Uninstalling

Remove the panel with your ZXP installer, or delete the CursorTime folder it installed. On a Mac it's in one of these:

/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/CursorTime
~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/CursorTime

On Windows, in one of these:

C:\Program Files (x86)\Common Files\Adobe\CEP\extensions\CursorTime
%APPDATA%\Adobe\CEP\extensions\CursorTime

Requirements

After Effects 2022 or later, on macOS or Windows. Cursor Time runs entirely inside After Effects: no accounts, no internet connection, nothing uploaded.
